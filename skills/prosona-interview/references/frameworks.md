# The five frameworks

Polysona used ten. Ten is right when the goal is a psychological portrait. The goal
here is narrower and more useful: **decision principles a later phase can execute**.
Five frameworks reach that, and each one is kept because it produces a different
kind of row in the persona file.

| # | Framework | What it yields | Lands in |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | McAdams life narrative (short form) | the problem types this planner keeps returning to | 판단 원칙 |
| F2 | Laddering | what "a good screen" actually means, down to the value | 판단 원칙 |
| F3 | 5 Whys | the root under a criterion they use constantly | 판단 원칙 |
| F4 | Contrast case | a product they admire but refuse to copy | 안티 골 |
| F5 | Failure post-mortem | a past call that turned out wrong | 맹점 |

F4 and F5 are not optional colour. A persona with only F1–F3 says what the planner
likes, and an agent that only knows what you like will agree with you. F4 gives it
something to refuse; F5 gives it a place to argue back.

## F1 생애 서사 (축약)

Two or three questions, not a life story. You are looking for the repeat.

- "지금까지 만든 것 중에 제일 오래 붙잡고 있었던 게 뭐예요?"
- "그거 말고 또 비슷하게 오래 붙잡았던 게 있어요? 둘의 공통점이 뭐라고 보세요?"
- "반대로, 시작했다가 금방 흥미가 식은 건요?"

The pair — what held and what did not — is the signal. One story alone is an anecdote.

## F2 래더링

Start from a concrete artifact, climb to the value. Never start from "무엇을 중요하게
생각하세요" — that question gets the answer they think they should give.

- "최근에 '이건 잘 만들었다' 싶었던 화면 하나만 떠올려 주세요. 어떤 거였어요?"
- "그 화면의 어떤 점이 그렇게 느끼게 했어요?" → attribute
- "그게 사용자한테 뭘 해주죠?" → consequence
- "그게 왜 중요해요?" → value

Stop when the answer stops changing. That last rung is the principle.

## F3 5 Whys

Pick a criterion they have already used twice in this interview and dig under it.

- "아까 '마찰이 적어야 한다'고 두 번 말씀하셨어요. 왜 그게 그렇게 중요해요?"
- (× 4, each on the previous answer)

If the third why produces a general truth ("사용자는 원래 참을성이 없으니까"), you
have left their judgment and entered common sense. Back up one rung and ask for a
case instead: "그 판단을 실제로 적용했던 때가 언제였어요?"

## F4 대조 사례

- "만듦새는 인정하는데 우리는 저렇게 안 했으면 좋겠다 싶은 제품이 있어요?"
- "구체적으로 어떤 부분이요?"
- "그럼 그 자리에 뭐가 들어가야 한다고 보세요?"

The third question matters. "저건 싫다"는 취향이고, "저 자리에 이게 와야 한다"가
안티 골이다.

## F5 실패 부검

Ask for a wrong call, not a failed project. Projects fail for funding and timing;
calls fail for reasons a persona file can carry.

- "돌이켜보면 틀렸던 기획 판단 하나만 얘기해 주실 수 있어요?"
- "그때는 왜 맞다고 생각하셨어요?"
- "그게 틀렸다는 건 언제, 어떻게 알았어요?"
- "같은 상황이 다시 오면 뭘 다르게 보시겠어요?"

The last answer is the 맹점 row. Write it as the place where the agent should push
back hardest, not as a confession.
